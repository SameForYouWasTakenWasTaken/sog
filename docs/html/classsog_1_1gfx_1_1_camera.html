Detailed Description

A movable, rotatable camera producing view and projection matrices.

The camera stores a position and Euler-style orientation (yaw, pitch, roll in degrees) plus a world-up reference that lets it work in any up-convention via an internal frame quaternion. Projection can be perspective (setPerspective) or orthographic (setOrthographic); the active type is remembered and the matching matrix is built on demand.

Both getView() and getProjection() cache their result and rebuild only after a corresponding mutator marks them dirty, so querying matrices every frame is cheap.

Movement comes in two flavours: move() translates in world space, while moveLocal() translates along the camera's own right/up/forward axes. lookAt() repositions and reorients the camera to gaze from eye toward target.

Note
Angles are in degrees. yaw == -90 and pitch == 0 (the defaults) make the camera look along -Z in the default frame.
