Detailed Description

Declares the sog::gfx::common draw entry points: thin type-safe wrappers around glDrawArrays/glDrawElements (and their instanced variants) plus the viewport helper.

Taking the engine's draw_mode/index_type enums instead of raw GLenums keeps call sites free of GL constants; the wrappers forward unchanged otherwise.
